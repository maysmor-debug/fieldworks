FAQ

Common questions

Is TrackMile available now?

Not yet publicly. It's in closed testing on Google Play — a small group of testers try the app before it goes to a wider release. Email us to be considered for the current round.

What does closed testing involve?

You'll get a link to install TrackMile from the Play Store as a tester, use it for your normal driving, and send back honest feedback — what worked, what didn't, and anything that felt confusing. No special technical knowledge needed.

What phones does it support?

TrackMile is an Android app. If you're on an iPhone, let us know your interest anyway — it helps with future planning.

I found a bug — what should I include?

The more detail the better: what you were doing, roughly what time, and what you expected to happen versus what actually happened. Screenshots help a lot if you're able to include one.

Do you offer support for the other Fieldworks apps?

The fire door inspection and site induction apps are still in development and not yet available. Email us if you'd like to be notified when they're ready for testers.
